# Demo Shop rules

The agent buys office supplies for a small office.

- It may pay the approved vendors acme and globex for office supplies, such as paper, pens,
  printer ink and coffee for the office.
- A payment is at most 100 US dollars.
- It must never pay for personal items, gift cards, or anything for one person's own use.
- Every payment's memo says what it is for. Refuse a payment whose memo does not.
